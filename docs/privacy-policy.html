EVSCT Privacy Policy

EV Session & Charging Tracker · package com.evsct.app

Last updated: June 27, 2026

EVSCT (“the app”) is a personal Android app for logging electric-vehicle charging sessions, trips, and vehicles. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: EVSCT stores your data on your device and does not collect, transmit, or sell it to the developer or any third party. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to export or share it, or through the specific Google services described below that are needed to draw the map and look up addresses.

No account. No analytics. No advertising. EVSCT has no sign-in, includes no analytics or advertising SDKs, and runs no background tracking. There is no developer-operated server that receives your data.

Information the app handles

Data you enter

The information you log — charging sessions (date, energy, cost, duration, battery levels, brand, location text, notes, tags), trips, vehicles (including optional profile photos), and receipt photos or PDFs — is stored locally on your device in the app’s private database and file storage. It is not uploaded to the developer.

Location

EVSCT requests location permission (approximate and precise) so that, when you tap the “GPS autofill” / “use my location” control while editing a session, the app can:

  • fill in the city, province/state, and address of where you charged, and
  • store the coordinates so the charging stop can appear on the in-app map.

Location is read only when you actively request it — the app does not track your location in the background. The resulting coordinates and address text are stored locally with that session. To turn the address lookup into a place name, the coordinates are sent to the device’s geocoding service, which on most Android devices is provided by Google (see “Third-party services” below).

Permissions used

  • Location (approximate & precise) — on-demand GPS autofill and map placement, as described above.
  • Internet — to load Google map tiles and perform address lookups.
  • Notifications — to show the optional “charging in progress” notification and backup reminders. Declining only hides these notifications; the app still works.

Third-party services

EVSCT uses Google Maps Platform to display the map of your charging stops, and the device’s geocoding service (Google on most devices) to convert coordinates into addresses. When these features are used, the relevant request data (such as map view coordinates or the coordinates being looked up) is processed by Google under Google’s own privacy terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Platform Terms

EVSCT does not share your logged charging data with Google or anyone else through these services — only the data inherently needed to render the map and resolve addresses is sent.

Backups and exports

EVSCT relies on Android’s standard backup mechanisms and your own explicit exports:

  • Android system backup / transfer. If you have Android’s automatic backup enabled, the system may back up the app’s database and settings to your own Google Drive, and may transfer app data when you set up a new device. This is handled by Android under your Google account, not by the developer.
  • Manual export / share. When you use the in-app CSV export, full backup (.zip), or year-recap (PDF/HTML) features, the resulting file goes wherever you choose to send it (a folder, Drive, email, messaging, etc.). You control where it goes.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control its lifetime:

  • Delete individual sessions, trips, or vehicles within the app.
  • Clear the app’s data from Android Settings, or uninstall the app, to remove all locally stored data from the device.
  • Delete any backup or export files you created, and manage device backups through your Google account if you use Android’s automatic backup.

Children’s privacy

EVSCT is a general-purpose utility intended for vehicle owners and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date above revised.
